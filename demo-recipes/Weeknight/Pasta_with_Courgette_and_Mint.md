# Pasta with courgette and mint

![[_resources/Pasta_with_Courgette_and_Mint.png]]

## Ingredients

- 300g spaghetti
- 3 courgettes, coarsely grated
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- A handful of mint leaves
- 40g parmesan, grated

## Method

1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water.
2. Meanwhile fry the garlic gently, then add the courgette and cook until soft and sweet, about 10 minutes.
3. Toss in the drained pasta with a splash of cooking water, the parmesan and torn mint.
