# Lemon and herb traybake chicken

![[_resources/Lemon_and_Herb_Traybake_Chicken.png]]

## Ingredients

- 8 chicken thighs, skin on
- 600g new potatoes, halved
- 1 lemon, quartered
- 1 bulb garlic, cloves separated
- A few sprigs of thyme
- 3 tbsp olive oil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
2. Toss everything in a large roasting tin with salt, pepper and the oil.
3. Roast for 45 minutes, turning the potatoes once, until the chicken skin is crisp.
4. Squeeze the roasted lemon over before serving.
