# Baked cod with chorizo and beans

![[_resources/Baked_Cod_with_Chorizo_and_Beans.png]]

## Ingredients

- 4 cod fillets
- 100g cooking chorizo, sliced
- 2 tins butter beans, drained
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- Chopped parsley

## Method

1. Fry the chorizo in an ovenproof pan until it releases its oil.
2. Add the tomatoes and beans and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Sit the cod on top and bake at 200°C for 12 minutes.
4. Scatter with parsley.
