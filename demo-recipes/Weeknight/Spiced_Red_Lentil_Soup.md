# Spiced red lentil soup

![[_resources/Spiced_Red_Lentil_Soup.png]]

## Ingredients

- 200g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- Juice of half a lemon

## Method

1. Soften the onion in a little oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add the cumin and carrot and cook for 2 minutes.
3. Add the lentils and stock, simmer for 20 minutes until soft.
4. Blend until smooth, season, and finish with lemon juice.
