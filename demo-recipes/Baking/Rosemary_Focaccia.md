# Rosemary focaccia

![[_resources/Rosemary_Focaccia.png]]

## Ingredients

- 500g strong white flour
- 7g fast-action yeast
- 400ml warm water
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- Rosemary sprigs and flaky salt

## Method

1. Mix the flour, yeast, water and 2 tbsp oil into a wet dough; rest for 15 minutes.
2. Stretch and fold four times over an hour.
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
4. Spread in an oiled tin, dimple, top with rosemary, salt and oil, and bake at 230°C for 20 minutes.
