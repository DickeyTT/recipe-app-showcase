# Lemon posset

![[_resources/Lemon_Posset.png]]

## Ingredients

- 600ml double cream
- 150g caster sugar
- Juice of 2 lemons

## Method

1. Bring the cream and sugar to the boil and simmer for 3 minutes.
2. Off the heat, stir in the lemon juice.
3. Pour into glasses and chill for at least 3 hours.
