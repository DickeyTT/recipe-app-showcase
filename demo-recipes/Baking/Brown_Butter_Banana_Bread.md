# Brown butter banana bread

![[_resources/Brown_Butter_Banana_Bread.png]]

## Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas
- 115g butter
- 200g plain flour
- 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 120g light brown sugar
- 2 eggs

## Method

1. Melt the butter and cook until it smells nutty and turns golden brown. Cool slightly.
2. Mash the bananas and beat in the butter, sugar and eggs.
3. Fold in the flour and bicarbonate.
4. Bake in a lined loaf tin at 175°C for 55 to 60 minutes.
