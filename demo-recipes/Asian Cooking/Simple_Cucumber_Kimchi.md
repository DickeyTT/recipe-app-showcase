# Simple cucumber kimchi

![[_resources/Simple_Cucumber_Kimchi.png]]

## Ingredients

- 4 small cucumbers
- 1 tbsp salt
- 2 tbsp gochugaru
- 3 spring onions, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, grated

## Method

1. Cut the cucumbers into batons, salt them and leave for 30 minutes.
2. Rinse and drain well.
3. Mix with the remaining ingredients and pack into a clean jar.
4. Eat after a day in the fridge; keeps for a week.
