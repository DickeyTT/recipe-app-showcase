# Thai green vegetable curry

![[_resources/Thai_Green_Vegetable_Curry.png]]

## Ingredients

- 1 aubergine, cubed
- 150g green beans
- 400ml coconut milk
- 2 tbsp green curry paste (check it has no shrimp paste)
- A handful of Thai basil

## Method

1. Fry the curry paste in a splash of the coconut milk until fragrant.
2. Add the rest of the coconut milk and the aubergine; simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Add the beans for the last 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the basil and serve with rice.
