# Quick pork stir-fry with greens

![[_resources/Quick_Pork_Stir_Fry_with_Greens.png]]

## Ingredients

- 300g pork mince
- 2 heads pak choi, quartered
- Thumb of ginger, grated
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- Steamed rice, to serve

## Method

1. Fry the pork in a hot wok until browned and crisp in places.
2. Add the ginger and garlic for 30 seconds.
3. Add the pak choi and soy sauce and toss until just wilted.
4. Serve over rice.
