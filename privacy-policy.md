# RecipeApp Clipper — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

RecipeApp Clipper is a Chrome extension that saves the recipe on a web page you are
viewing as a Markdown bundle on your own computer, so you can import it into
RecipeApp, a personal offline recipe library.

## What the extension does with data

- When you click the extension on a recipe page, it reads the content of that one
  page (title, ingredients, method, photos and surrounding notes) in order to
  build the recipe bundle.
- It saves that bundle (a Markdown file, its images and a small sidecar file) to
  your Downloads folder.
- All of this happens on your own device.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.
- It does not send the page content, your files or any usage information to the
  developer or to any server or third party.
- It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not read any page until you click the extension on it.

## Permissions

- **activeTab** and **scripting** are used only when you click the extension, to
  read the recipe from the current page.
- **downloads** is used to save the recipe files to your Downloads folder.
